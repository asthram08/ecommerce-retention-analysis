# Findings

## Q1: Do new shoppers come back?

- 92% of new shoppers do not return the next week. Week 1 retention is 8%.
- Retention keeps falling (5% in week 2, 3% by week 4), then flattens at about 2% from week 7 on. That 2% is a small loyal core.
- Holiday shoppers are the least loyal. The Dec 16 and Dec 23 cohorts had the lowest week 1 retention (7.0% and 5.9%), and December cohorts end near 1.3 to 1.5% by weeks 10 to 12, vs 2.0 to 2.4% for early November.
- Black Friday pulled people back: the Nov 18 cohort had the best week 1 retention (10.2%).

**So what:** the store gets first visits but not second ones. The first 7 days after a first visit is the biggest opportunity.
